GLYPH TUNER

Load the .png and .json a build produced. Click a glyph, then ↑/↓ to adjust it by one device pixel (hold Shift for five). Move shifts the glyph along the vertical axis. Stretch scales it vertically about the baseline, leaving width and stroke weight alone. Both are draw-time values, so what you see here is exactly what the runtime produces.
